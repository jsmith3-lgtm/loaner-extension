Both at once. Every frame is searched for every code it contains, so one steady hold fills both slots. Either Lenovo QR code gives the serial — the plain one is trimmed to its first 8 characters, the warranty URL has its sn= value read out — and anything else becomes the asset tag. Boxes drawn on the picture show what's being read: green is a code that just went into a slot, yellow is one already recorded, red means two devices are in shot at once. The strip under the picture is the live tally.

After a save the reader locks until the device leaves the frame, so the same labels aren't counted twice.

The name field is off by default. Set it to optional or required and mark where the name is printed, and it is read once both codes are captured. OCR has no error correction, so it always proposes rather than decides — nothing is saved until you press Enter. Paste your list of known names and readings are matched against it, which is far more reliable than trusting the characters.
